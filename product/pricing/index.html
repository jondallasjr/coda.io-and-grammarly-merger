Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our pricing? We have answers.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle, and we'll prorate any differences.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Absolutely. All plans come with a 20% discount when you choose annual billing. That's equivalent to getting 2.4 months free!

Is there a free trial?

Yes, all plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to features. No credit card required to get started.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), as well as payment via invoice for Business plans.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we'll issue a full refund. After that, you can cancel anytime with no penalties.

What's included in your support?

Starter includes email support during business hours. Professional adds priority support. Business includes 24/7 phone and email support with a dedicated account manager.
