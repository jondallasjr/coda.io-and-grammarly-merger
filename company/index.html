About Coda + Grammarly

Uniting collaboration and communication to empower teams worldwide

Our Mission

We're building the future of work by combining powerful document collaboration with intelligent writing assistance. Together, Coda and Grammarly create a unified platform where teams can think, write, and communicate more effectively.

Our Story

Coda's Foundation

Founded in 2014, Coda reimagined what documents could be—transforming them from static files into living, interactive workspaces where teams collaborate in real-time.

Grammarly's Evolution

Since 2009, Grammarly has been the trusted writing assistant for millions, leveraging AI to help people communicate clearly and confidently across all platforms.

A Natural Convergence

In 2025, these two industry leaders merged to create something unprecedented—a platform that seamlessly combines structured collaboration with intelligent communication.
